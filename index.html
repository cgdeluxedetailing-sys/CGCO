THE IDEA BEHIND CG CO.

A LOCAL
APPROACH.

Built around doing the job right.

CG Co. is the shared name connecting a growing group of independent service businesses in Gippsland. Each business operates in its own field, with its own customers and its own approach.

The idea is simple: bring them together under one recognisable name while keeping the specialist service and personal attention that makes each business its own.
